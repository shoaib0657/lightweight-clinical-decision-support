# Clinician discharge handoff

This synthetic handoff template asks the discharging team to state the reason for care, the important findings, the working assessment recorded by the responsible clinician, completed procedures, relevant medicine changes without reproducing medication doses, pending tests, and required follow-up. Each pending item should have a named owner and a route for communicating the result. Record warning signs, escalation contacts, and any barriers that could prevent the follow-up plan from being completed. The receiving clinician should be able to distinguish completed actions from recommendations and unresolved questions.

# Patient discharge explanation

The patient-facing explanation uses short sentences and familiar terms. It describes what happened during the encounter, what the care team has asked the person to do next, how to obtain follow-up, and which warning signs require contacting the appropriate clinical service. Medicine information should match the approved discharge record and must not be invented from this fixture. Ask the person or caregiver to explain the plan back in their own words, provide language or accessibility support when needed, and document questions that remain unanswered. This educational text is not a substitute for individualized discharge instructions.
